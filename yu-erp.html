About Yu-ERP

Yu-ERP is Yugarit's all-in-one Business & Operations SaaS platform that unifies the four pillars of any growing organisation — CRM, HR & Payroll, Project Management, and Inventory — into a single, tightly integrated system. Instead of juggling five different tools with disconnected data and manual handoffs between teams, organisations on Yu-ERP operate from one centralised platform where every department shares a live, consistent view of the business. Whether you are a 20-person startup formalising your operations or a 500-person enterprise modernising a legacy ERP stack, Yu-ERP scales with you — delivering enterprise-grade functionality at SaaS speed and pricing.

The Problem We Set Out to Solve

Growing businesses accumulate operational debt quietly. Sales teams manage leads in spreadsheets. HR runs payroll in a standalone tool that doesn't talk to attendance records. Project managers track milestones in one app while finance tracks budgets in another. Procurement manages stock in a separate system with no link to sales orders or project requirements. The result is a business that generates data in silos — creating reconciliation nightmares, delayed decisions, and costly errors at every handoff point. Traditional ERP systems promise integration but deliver complexity: months-long implementations, expensive consultants, and interfaces that staff refuse to use. Yu-ERP was built to change that equation — a modern, intuitive ERP that deploys in days, not months, and gets adopted because it genuinely makes every team's daily work easier.

The integration across Yu-ERP's modules is what makes it genuinely different. When a deal is closed in the CRM, it can automatically trigger a project in the Project Management module, raise a procurement request in Inventory, and onboard resources against the project in HR — without a single manual data entry step. Payroll pulls directly from attendance and leave records. Project budgets link to actual inventory and resource costs in real time. Management dashboards synthesise data across all four modules, giving leadership a single number for revenue, headcount cost, project health, and stock levels simultaneously — from any device, anywhere.

Business Impact & Operational Outcomes

Organisations deploying Yu-ERP report significant improvements in operational efficiency within the first quarter. Finance teams eliminate an average of 18 hours per month in manual cross-system reconciliation. Sales cycles shorten by 25% through automated follow-up sequences, pipeline visibility, and faster quotation-to-order conversion. Project delivery teams report a 32% improvement in on-time completion rates driven by real-time resource allocation visibility and automated escalation workflows when milestones are at risk.

Core Platform Modules

CRM & Sales

Lead capture, pipeline management, automated follow-up sequences, quotation and proposal generation, and deal-to-project conversion — all in one flow. Full customer history from first contact to post-sale support.

HR & Payroll

Employee onboarding, attendance and leave management, performance appraisals, and fully automated payroll with statutory deductions (PF, ESI, TDS) and payslip generation — compliant with Indian labour law.

Project Management

Gantt charts, task boards, milestone tracking, resource allocation, budget vs. actuals, and automated escalation alerts. Full project lifecycle from scoping to delivery, with client-facing portals for transparent reporting.

Inventory & Supply Chain

Multi-warehouse stock management, purchase order automation, supplier management, goods receipt, and real-time stock valuation. Demand-linked procurement triggers ensure stock levels stay aligned with active project and sales requirements.

Automation & Workflows

No-code workflow builder to automate approvals, notifications, task assignments, and cross-module triggers. Set rules once — the platform handles the follow-through, eliminating manual coordination between departments.

Analytics & Executive Dashboard

Real-time KPI dashboards across all modules — revenue pipeline, headcount costs, project health scores, and inventory turnover in one view. Customisable reports scheduled for automatic delivery to leadership inboxes.
