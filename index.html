Frequently Ask Question

Have Questions in Your Mind? Get the Answers Now

What types of IT services does Yugarit offer?

Yugarit offers a comprehensive range of IT services including custom software development, mobile app development, UI/UX design, digital marketing & SEO, cloud migration, data analytics, and cybersecurity. Our solutions are tailored to meet the unique needs of each client, whether you’re a startup or an enterprise.

How long does a typical software development project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A standard web application typically takes 6–12 weeks, while a full enterprise platform may require 3–6 months. We begin every engagement with a detailed discovery phase to define clear milestones and ensure on-time delivery without compromising quality.

Is my data secure when migrating to the cloud?

Absolutely. Security is our top priority during any cloud migration. We follow industry-standard encryption protocols, conduct vulnerability assessments before and after migration, and implement multi-layered access controls. Our team ensures zero data loss and minimal downtime throughout the entire process.

Do you provide post-launch support and maintenance?

Yes, we provide ongoing support and maintenance for all our solutions. Our dedicated support team is available 24/7 to address bugs, performance issues, and feature updates. We offer flexible support packages — from basic bug fixes to full managed service agreements — so you’re always covered after go-live.
