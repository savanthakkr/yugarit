About Yu-Commerce

Yu-Commerce is Yugarit's flagship SaaS-based eCommerce platform — purpose-built for businesses that need a fully customizable, scalable online store without the overhead of managing infrastructure. Whether you're a D2C brand launching your first store, a multi-vendor marketplace, or an enterprise with complex catalogue requirements, Yu-Commerce adapts to your model. It ships with a headless storefront, a powerful seller dashboard, native payment gateway integrations, and an extensible plugin marketplace — all accessible from a single cloud-hosted subscription.

The Problem We Set Out to Solve

Most eCommerce platforms force businesses into a rigid template: limited customisation, vendor lock-in, or expensive enterprise contracts that take months to onboard. Small and mid-size businesses in India especially struggle to find a platform that is both affordable and genuinely scalable — one that grows with them from 100 orders a month to 100,000 without a platform migration. Yu-Commerce was designed to close that gap. Built on a microservices architecture hosted on AWS India, it gives every merchant full control over their storefront, inventory logic, pricing rules, and checkout experience — with zero-downtime deployments and pay-as-you-grow pricing.

The platform was designed mobile-first, with a React-based headless frontend that loads in under 1.5 seconds on a 4G connection. Merchants get a drag-and-drop page builder, real-time inventory sync across channels, and built-in SEO tools. The API-first architecture means Yu-Commerce connects seamlessly with ERP systems, CRMs, logistics providers, and third-party analytics — without writing a single line of custom integration code.

Platform Capabilities & Results

Since launch, Yu-Commerce has onboarded over 120 merchants across retail, fashion, electronics, and food & beverage verticals. Early adopters report an average 34% increase in conversion rate compared to their previous platforms, driven by faster load times, one-click checkout, and personalised product recommendations powered by the built-in ML engine. The platform handles peak-season traffic spikes of up to 40× baseline load with auto-scaling ECS clusters — without any manual intervention from the merchant.

  • Headless React storefront with sub-1.5s load time on mobile

  • Multi-vendor marketplace support with independent seller dashboards

  • Native integrations: Razorpay, PayU, Shiprocket, Unicommerce, and GST-ready invoicing

  • Auto-scaling infrastructure handling 40× traffic spikes with zero downtime

  • 34% average increase in merchant conversion rates post-migration

  • 120+ merchants onboarded across retail, fashion, and F&B verticals

Core Platform Features

Customisable Storefronts

Drag-and-drop page builder with 50+ pre-built blocks. Full theme customisation — no developer required. White-label options available for agencies and resellers.

Inventory & Order Management

Real-time inventory sync across online and offline channels. Bulk import/export, variant management, and automated low-stock alerts built in.

Payment & Checkout

One-click checkout, saved addresses, and 15+ payment methods including UPI, cards, wallets, and BNPL. PCI-DSS compliant with 3D Secure support.

Analytics & Insights

Real-time sales dashboard, customer lifetime value tracking, abandoned cart recovery, and cohort analysis — all without third-party tools.

API-First Architecture

REST and GraphQL APIs for every platform resource. Connect your ERP, CRM, logistics platform, or any third-party tool without custom code.

Security & Compliance

SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure. Automatic SSL, DDoS protection, GDPR-ready data handling, and full GST-compliant invoicing for Indian merchants.
