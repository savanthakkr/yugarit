About Yu-Learn

Yu-Learn is Yugarit's modern Learning Management System — a cloud-hosted SaaS platform purpose-built for organisations, educational institutes, and independent trainers who need a single, powerful environment to create, manage, and deliver engaging online learning experiences. From a corporate L&D team rolling out compliance training to a coaching institute running live batches, or a solo educator monetising their expertise, Yu-Learn provides everything needed to build a world-class digital learning operation — without the complexity or cost of stitching together multiple tools.

The Problem We Set Out to Solve

Education and training delivery has shifted online — but most organisations still rely on a patchwork of disconnected tools: a video conferencing app for live classes, a Google Drive folder for study materials, a spreadsheet for tracking attendance, a third-party quiz tool for assessments, and email for certificates. This fragmentation creates administrative overhead, inconsistent learner experiences, and zero visibility into whether the training is actually working. Yu-Learn replaces this entire stack with one integrated platform — giving administrators, instructors, and learners a cohesive experience from day one, while giving management the analytics they need to measure outcomes and improve programme effectiveness continuously.

Yu-Learn is built around the instructor-learner relationship at its core. Instructors use an intuitive course builder to structure content across modules, upload videos, PDFs, quizzes, and assignments, and schedule live sessions — all within a single workflow. Learners get a clean, distraction-free dashboard showing enrolled courses, upcoming live classes, pending assignments, and their progress at a glance. The platform supports self-paced learning, instructor-led cohort batches, and blended formats — making it equally suitable for asynchronous corporate training and live classroom-style teaching.

Learning Outcomes & Platform Impact

Organisations deploying Yu-Learn report measurable improvements in both operational efficiency and learner outcomes. Training administrators save an average of 12 hours per week by eliminating manual enrolment, attendance tracking, and certificate generation. Course completion rates on Yu-Learn are 47% higher compared to the same content delivered through email-based distribution, driven by progress nudges, gamified milestones, and the structured learning path experience. Certification programmes report a 91% pass rate among learners who complete the full course path — a direct result of the built-in formative assessment and gap-identification engine.

  • 47% higher course completion rates vs. email-based content distribution

  • Auto-generated certificates with custom branding and verifiable QR codes

  • Learner analytics — progress, time-on-task, assessment scores, and drop-off points

Core Platform Features

Course Creation & Content Management

Drag-and-drop course builder with support for video lectures, PDFs, slideshows, SCORM packages, and embedded content. Organise learning into structured modules and lessons with prerequisite controls and drip scheduling.

Live Classes & Webinars

Integrated live class scheduling with in-platform video conferencing, real-time attendance tracking, breakout rooms, polls, and automatic session recording — available to enrolled learners on demand post-session.

Student Enrolment & Management

Bulk enrolment via CSV, self-registration with approval workflows, and cohort-based batch management. Role-based access for admins, instructors, and learners — with detailed profiles and learning histories.

Assessments & Quizzes

Flexible question builder supporting MCQs, fill-in-the-blank, short answer, and file-upload assignments. Timed assessments, randomised question banks, auto-grading, and manual instructor review — all in one place.

Certification & Badges

Auto-generate branded certificates on course completion or assessment pass. Verifiable QR codes on every certificate. Digital badges for milestone achievements that learners can share on LinkedIn and social profiles.

Analytics & Progress Tracking

Real-time learner dashboards showing course progress, time-on-task, assessment scores, and completion status. Admin-level reports on cohort performance, drop-off points, and overall programme effectiveness.
