About The Project Overview

The client approached us with a clear challenge: their existing platform could no longer handle the rapid growth in user traffic and transaction volume. Legacy architecture was causing performance bottlenecks and making new feature deployments costly and slow.

The Project Challenge

Our team conducted a thorough technical audit of the existing system, identifying key bottlenecks in the database layer and API infrastructure. We proposed a phased migration to a microservices architecture hosted on AWS, with zero-downtime deployment pipelines.

The result was a platform that handles 5x the original traffic with 99.97% uptime. Deployment cycles went from two weeks to under four hours, and the client's development team can now ship features independently across services.

The Result Of Our Project

The result was a platform that handles 5x the original traffic with 99.97% uptime. Deployment cycles went from two weeks to under four hours, and the client's development team can now ship features independently across services.

  • Reduced infrastructure costs by 42% through cloud-native architecture

  • Deployed CI/CD pipelines cutting release cycles from 14 days to 4 hours

  • Achieved 99.97% platform uptime post-migration

  • Scaled to 500,000 concurrent users without performance degradation
