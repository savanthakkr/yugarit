UI/UX Design

At Yugarit, our UI/UX design process starts with understanding your users deeply — their goals, frustrations, and behaviours. We conduct user research, competitive analysis, and stakeholder interviews to ensure every design decision is grounded in evidence, not assumptions.

From wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes, we take your product through a rigorous design sprint. Our designers work hand-in-hand with your development team, delivering pixel-perfect specs and interactive prototypes that dramatically reduce development rework and time-to-market.

  • User research & persona development

  • Wireframing and interactive prototyping

  • Accessibility-first design (WCAG 2.1 compliant)

  • Design system creation and documentation

Quality Full Work

Our designs are tested with real users before a single line of code is written, ensuring your product solves the right problems the right way.

100% Work Satisfication

We deliver complete design systems — not just screens — so your team can build consistently at speed.

What does your UI/UX design process look like?

We follow a research-first approach: discovery and user research, information architecture, wireframing, high-fidelity design, and usability testing. Each stage is collaborative with your team to ensure alignment throughout.

How long does a UI/UX design project take?

A typical UI/UX engagement runs 4–10 weeks depending on scope. A simple app redesign may take 4 weeks, while a full product design system for a complex platform can take 10–12 weeks. We provide a clear timeline estimate after the discovery session.

Do you also handle the development after design?

Yes — Yugarit offers end-to-end services. After the design is approved, our engineering team can build the product using the same design system. This tight designer-developer collaboration eliminates handoff friction and speeds up delivery.

Will I own all the design files and assets?

Absolutely. All design files, assets, and prototypes are fully owned by you upon project completion. We deliver organised Figma or Adobe XD source files along with a comprehensive design handoff document.
