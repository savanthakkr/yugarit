Cloud Migration & Software Architecture Case Study

NexaCommerce — Arjun Mehta, CTO

NexaCommerce was running a monolithic e-commerce platform that could not scale beyond 50,000 concurrent users. Yugarit designed and delivered a full cloud migration to AWS with microservices architecture, reducing infrastructure costs by 42% and achieving 99.97% uptime within 60 days of go-live.

Category: Cloud Migration & Software Architecture
Client: Arjun Mehta, CTO at NexaCommerce

NexaCommerce's legacy platform had become a bottleneck — peak-season traffic caused repeated outages and the team could not ship features without a 3-week release cycle. Yugarit's engineering team performed a full architectural assessment, designed a 12-service microservices decomposition, and migrated the entire workload to AWS ECS with zero downtime.

  • 42% reduction in monthly cloud infrastructure costs post-migration

  • CI/CD pipeline live within 60 days — release cycle cut from 3 weeks to 2 days

  • 99.97% uptime sustained over 6 months with auto-scaling ECS clusters

The engagement spanned 14 weeks from discovery to production. Yugarit provided dedicated solution architects, two full-stack squads, and a DevOps lead. Post-launch support included a 90-day hypercare period with 24/7 monitoring and on-call SLA.
